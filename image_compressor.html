❓ Dual Compression FAQ

What is the Compression Strength Bar?

It's a separate control that amplifies compression. Strength 1 = mild, Strength 10 = very aggressive, giving you extra reduction beyond the quality bar.

How does it interact with Quality?

Final quality = Quality% / (strength * 0.2 + 0.8) — dynamic mapping ensures strength dramatically lowers file size.

Which format works best?

WebP and JPEG respond excellently to strength changes. PNG is lossless but still compresses well.

Where is the action button?

The "Apply Dual Compression" button is now placed directly below the Compression Strength Bar for better workflow.
